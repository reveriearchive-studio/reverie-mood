The Official Privacy Statement of

Reverie Mood

Your Private Emotional Gazette
Last Updated · October 27, 2026

At a Glance

"Your journal stays on your device. Only your own backup copy and what you choose to share travel through Apple's cloud."

  • All journal data stored locally on your device
  • No servers, no analytics, no tracking
  • Shared features use Apple's iCloud only
  • Subscriptions processed entirely by Apple
  • No advertisements, ever

Our Philosophy

Reverie Mood is a privacy-first mood journal by Reverie Archive. Your emotional reflections are yours alone. Everything you write, record, sketch, and feel stays on your device, and in your own private iCloud Drive while the iCloud Mirror is on (see iCloud & Backups below). It reaches another person only if you explicitly choose to share it with a friend through the Shared Cove.

What Stays on Your Device

The vast majority of Reverie Mood lives on your device (the iCloud Mirror, described below, can also keep a private copy of your backups, photos, doodles, and voice memos in your own iCloud Drive):

  • Mood entries, written reflections, and voice memos
  • Photos and doodles attached to entries
  • Breathing and grounding exercises — activity records and progress
  • Letters to the Ether — private postcards you compose, keep, release, or burn
  • Ambient sounds and comfort affirmations — usage preferences
  • Companion data — your Puff's name and your display name
  • Subscription status — your Reverie+ entitlement, stored locally for offline access
  • App settings — all your preferences and customizations

What Travels Through iCloud

Two features in The Quiet Room send data beyond your device — and only through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit). We operate no servers of our own. (The iCloud Mirror, which keeps your own backup copy in your private iCloud Drive, is described under iCloud & Backups.)

The Shared Cove

Exchange handwritten-style letters with a close friend. You create a private cove by sending your friend an invitation, either a QR code or a link, and letters drift between you like messages in bottles. Anyone who has the invitation can use it, so send it only to the person you mean to invite.

Encrypted by Apple in transit and at rest

Letters and whisper replies are stored in iCloud (CloudKit), in the space shared between you and the friend you invite, and that is how they reach each other. Apple's servers encrypt them in transit and at rest. They are not end-to-end encrypted. We operate no servers and have no way to read them.

Display names, timestamps, delivery status, emoji reactions, and opt-in mood snapshots are stored in iCloud in the same way. A letter can also carry a small photo stamp, a picture you choose or take for that letter. If Mood sharing is on for that cove, it also carries a small tag with the mood you logged that day, which your friend sees beside the letter. These travel with the letter and are stored in iCloud like the rest. Reverie Mood never reads your real name or Apple ID; the only name in a cove is the display name you type.

Keepsakes. If you save a stamp from a letter to your Keepsakes, a copy is also stored in your own private iCloud: the stamp picture, a short excerpt of the letter, and the cove's name and emoji. Only your Apple account can read it, and removing the keepsake removes it.

Shoreline Rituals

Gentle daily intentions you share with a friend — synced through the same iCloud zone as your Shared Cove. Ritual data includes the category (e.g. "walking", "reading"), duration, and daily check-ins with optional short notes. This data is stored the same way and is not end-to-end encrypted.

Your Controls

You decide what's shared:

  • Mood sharing — off by default. You opt in per cove. When it is on, your friend can see your daily mood and the mood tag on your letters.
  • Read receipts — can be toggled off per cove
  • Leave Cove — if you created the cove, leaving permanently deletes every letter, reaction, whisper, and ritual in it from iCloud, for both of you. If you joined a friend's cove, leaving removes it from your device; your friend keeps their copy.
  • Block — leaves the cove, as described above, and stops that person from connecting with you again on this device

Reverie+

Reverie Mood offers an optional premium tier. Here's what you should know:

  • All payments are handled by Apple. We never see your credit card, billing address, or Apple ID credentials.
  • Plans are monthly and annual, and the annual plan starts with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. A one-time lifetime purchase is offered only at certain times, such as the launch and the 2026 holidays; if you buy it, it stays yours and can be restored.
  • We store your entitlement locally — just whether you're on the free or premium tier — so the app works offline.
  • Early adopters who journaled before Reverie+ was introduced were given a complimentary 30-day courtesy period, detected entirely on your device.
  • Manage or cancel anytime at Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions.

Reverie Mood uses Apple's Standard EULA for all in-app purchases.

iCloud & Backups

Reverie Mood offers an in-app backup to your private iCloud Drive, called the iCloud Mirror. On a new install it starts switched on, and the first time you have a photo, doodle, or voice memo to keep, the app tells you so; you can switch it off at any time in Preferences, under Backup Manager. A phone that already had Reverie Mood keeps the choice it had, which was off until you switched it on. The mirror copies your backups and your photos, doodles, and voice memos into a folder named Reverie Mood in your iCloud Drive, which you can also open in the Files app. These copies are stored in your personal Apple account and are not accessible to us.

When you delete a photo, doodle, or voice memo from your journal, the app removes its copy from the mirror the next time it can reach iCloud. Making a photo smaller on your phone, or keeping an entry as text only, frees room on the phone and leaves the full-size copy in the mirror.

Dispatch a Copy. In Preferences you can make a file of your journal, with the words only or with the pictures too, and send it wherever you choose, such as Files, Mail, or AirDrop. The app makes the file on your phone. We never receive it, and it goes only where you send it.

If you have device-level iCloud Backup enabled, Reverie Mood data may also be included, except photos, doodles, and voice memos, which the app keeps out of device backups. You can manage this at Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Backups.

Deleting Your Data

You're always in control:

  • Delete individual entries, voice memos, photos, and doodles from the archive
  • Delete Letters to the Ether postcards, or let released ones expire after 180 days
  • Delete iCloud Drive backups from the backup manager
  • Leave a Shared Cove you created to permanently erase every letter, reaction, whisper, and ritual in it from iCloud, for both of you. If you joined a friend's cove, leaving removes it from your device and your friend keeps their copy
  • Remove a Keepsake to delete its copy from your private iCloud
  • Cancel your subscription through Apple ID settings — access continues until the billing period ends, then your local entitlement is cleared
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on your device. Copies kept in iCloud (the iCloud Mirror, Shared Cove data, and Keepsakes) stay there until you delete them

What We Don't Do

  • Operate servers — shared features use Apple's iCloud only
  • Use analytics or tracking of any kind
  • Share, sell, or monetize your data
  • Display advertisements
  • See or store your payment information
  • Read your letters or whispers

Children's Privacy

Reverie Mood is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Reverie Mood is not a medical app. It's a personal wellness journal. If you're experiencing mental health concerns, please reach out to a qualified professional.

Third-Party Services

Reverie Mood uses Apple's native frameworks — SwiftData, AVFoundation, CloudKit, and StoreKit — which are subject to Apple's Privacy Policy. No other third-party services are used.
