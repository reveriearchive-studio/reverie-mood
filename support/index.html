Frequently asked

Is there a guide inside the app?

Yes. Open Preferences, then Help Desk. It has a short guide to everything in the app, replays the welcome, and lets you turn the little tips on or off.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone. There is no Reverie account and no server holding your entries. The iCloud Mirror (Preferences, under Backup Manager) copies your backups, photos, doodles and voice memos into your own iCloud Drive, in a folder named Reverie Mood that also shows in the Files app. It starts on for a new install, and a phone that had Reverie Mood before keeps the choice it had. You can switch it on or off at any time.

How do I back up or move my entries?

Your phone saves a daily snapshot of your journal on its own. To keep a copy off the phone, make sure iCloud Mirror is on in Backup Manager and let it finish. On a new iPhone, install Reverie Mood and choose “I have a backup to restore” on the welcome page, or open Preferences, then Backup Manager, choose a backup and tap Restore. You can also use DISPATCH A COPY in Preferences to make your journal into one file, words only or with pictures, and Manual Import on the new phone to bring it back. Restoring is always a step you take yourself; it never happens on its own. Photos, doodles and voice memos are kept out of ordinary iPhone backups, so the iCloud Mirror is how they travel to a new phone.

What is Reverie+?

Reverie+ is an optional upgrade that adds extras, such as doodles, more photos and voice memos, the full library of exercises and sounds, more coves, time capsules and PDF export. The App Store page shows what is included and the current price. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled any time in your Apple ID settings.

How does The Shared Cove work?

The Shared Cove is a private space where two people exchange letters in their own time. Invite one person with a link or a QR code. You both need to be signed in to iCloud; there are no other accounts and no profiles.

How do reminders work?

Daily reminders are off until you say yes. The app offers a quiet evening reminder once or twice, never right after a heavy mood, and stops for good if you choose No thanks. You can change or stop it any time in Preferences, under Daily Dispatch. Once you allow notifications, Daily Dispatch also has Quiet Check-ins: if a week, two weeks or three weeks pass without you opening the app, a soft note may arrive. It is on by default, and you can switch it off there.

Is Reverie Mood a mental health service?

No. Reverie Mood is a personal journaling and well-being app, not a replacement for professional care.
